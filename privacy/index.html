Privacy policy

Last updated 16 October 2026

In short: the myikka app keeps everything on your phone. It has no internet permission, so it can't send your data to us or to anyone else, and we never receive it.

This policy covers the myikka Android app and this website, myikka.github.io. Both are run by the developer of myikka. Questions about privacy: dev864392@gmail.com.

What the app reads and keeps on your phone

The app stores the following in its private storage on your phone, which other apps can't read. None of it is sent anywhere.

Bank SMS (only if you allow it)

  • Reading SMS is the only permission the app asks for, and it is optional. The app asks only when you tap "Allow SMS access", and works without it: you can add spends from statements or by hand.
  • If you allow it, the app asks Android only for messages from the sender names of the banks whose cards you added. Of those, it uses only card purchase and refund alerts for your cards. OTPs, account alerts, offers and all your other messages are ignored and never stored.
  • From each purchase or refund alert it keeps the amount, the merchant, the date and time, the card's last 4 digits and how you paid (for example UPI). It also keeps a fingerprint of the message (a one-way code that can't be turned back into the text) so the same message is never counted twice. It never keeps the message text.
  • If you teach the app a new SMS format, it keeps the format (fixed words, with placeholders for the amount, merchant and date), not your message.

Card statements (only when you open one)

  • You choose a statement PDF with Android's file picker, or share it to the app. The app reads its lines, totals, dates, amount due and rewards. It doesn't keep the PDF.
  • If the PDF has a password and you tick "Remember", the password is kept encrypted with a key held by your phone's Android Keystore, so the next statement opens without typing it. Otherwise it is used once and forgotten.

What you enter

Your cards (name, bank, last 4 digits, membership and statement dates), categories, transactions you add or change, your answers to the app's checks, sale offers and your own reward rules.

App lock

If you turn it on, the app asks for your phone's own screen lock. Android checks it; the app never sees your PIN, pattern or fingerprint.

What can leave your phone

Nothing leaves your phone by itself. These happen only when you choose them:

  • Backup: the app writes an encrypted backup file (scrypt and AES-256-GCM) to a place you pick. Without your passphrase nobody can read it, and nobody can recover a forgotten passphrase, including us.
  • Add to calendar: opens your calendar app with an event "Pay <card name> bill" on the due date, with no amount or card digits. Your calendar app may sync it to your account.
  • Copy buttons (for example dispute details for your bank) put text on your phone's clipboard for you to paste wherever you choose.
  • Links: if the app opens this website, it opens in your browser, and the link carries nothing about you.

Android's own cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are turned off for the app, so its data isn't copied to Google's servers or to another phone.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • The app's data stays on your phone until you delete it. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in your phone's settings, deletes all of it. Inside the app you can also delete a statement you imported.
  • Backup files are yours: delete them where you saved them.
  • We hold no data about the app's users, so there is nothing for us to delete.

This website

  • The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub logs each visitor's IP address for security, as described in GitHub's privacy statement. We don't see or use these logs.
  • The site sets no cookies, has no analytics and loads nothing from other sites. Its only script is its own: it highlights the menu as you scroll, and reads, stores and sends nothing.

Tips

Tips are optional and go through a payment service (Razorpay) or your own UPI app, which handle your payment details under their own privacy policies. We receive what they pass on: for example your name, the amount, the date, a payment reference and, through Razorpay, the contact details you give it. We use these only to keep accounts, as the law requires, and to return a mistaken payment. They are kept for as long as tax law requires and are never linked to the app.

E-mail

If you write to us, we use your address and message only to reply. We delete them when you ask, unless the law requires us to keep them. Please never send card numbers, OTPs or passwords.

Children

myikka is meant for adults who hold credit cards. It isn't directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new date.
