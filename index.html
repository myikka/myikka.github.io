Statement check

Open your statement PDF and every line is matched with your bank's SMS. Payments with no SMS, and SMS missing from the statement, are listed.

Reward check

The rewards your bank showed, checked against the card's rules, with the likely cause of any difference and details to send your bank.

Insights

Where your money goes, and what each card really saved after fees, from your bank's own figures.

Sale offers

Add a bank offer from the checkout page during a sale, and "Which card?" counts the discount and its limits.

Bill reminders

A reminder before each due date from your statement, which you can add to your own calendar.

Locked and backed up

An app lock with your phone's own screen lock, and encrypted backups saved wherever you choose.

How it works

Set up once. Ask before you pay.

  1. Add your cards

    Pick your cards from the list, or add your own. No account, no sign-up.

  2. Bring in your spends

    Allow SMS so the app reads your banks' card alerts, or import your statement PDFs.

  3. Ask before you pay

    The app opens on "Which credit card to use?": pick how and what you're paying for, and pay with the best card.

The name

Why “myikka”?

In Hindi, the ace is the ikka, and hukum ka ikka is the ace of trumps: the card that wins the hand. We say it about the one thing we can always count on.

For every payment, one card in your wallet is that ace. One pays the most on food orders, another on fuel, a third during a sale. Most of us pay with whichever card comes out first, and the reward quietly slips away.

myikka finds the ace for you, one payment at a time. It's my ikka because it works only for you: your cards and your spends, on your phone.
